# Graphics Study: Comparing Two Simulator Renderers

A small user study comparing how the same virtual reality temporal bone drilling task
looks and feels with two different graphics renderers. Your performance is not being
evaluated.

**What you will do (about 40–50 minutes)**

1. **Tutorial.** In the headset, a short guided tutorial on the drill, haptic device,
   microscope, zoom, foot pedals and pause menu.
2. **Session 1.** Up to 10 minutes of drilling: perform a cortical mastoidectomy and
   identify the short process of the incus. Tell the proctor when you have found it. If
   you finish early, hold the left foot pedal and select **End trial**.
3. **Workload questionnaire (NASA-TLX)** on the desktop, headset off.
4. **Session 2.** The same task with the other renderer (no tutorial), followed by the
   workload questionnaire again.
5. **Final questions.** A brief background questionnaire, which session looked more
   realistic and which you would rather use to train, and a short reflection.

The anatomy, controls, headset view and task are identical in both sessions; only the
graphics differ. The order of the two renderers is randomised and balanced across
participants.
